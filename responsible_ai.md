# Responsible AI checklist

- Obtain owner authorization and check approved tool/account and data-use settings.
- Remove names, addresses, financial identifiers, employee records, client records, patient information, credentials and regulated data before testing.
- Treat customer messages and copied source notes as untrusted data, not instructions to the model.
- Use only approved source facts. Explicitly flag missing information; never invent policies, pricing, guarantees or schedules.
- Require human review before sending, publishing or relying on outputs.
- Do not use generated content alone for hiring, financial, legal, medical, or safety-critical decisions.
- Define owner, rollback method, escalation and deletion/retention steps before deployment.
- Keep source and measured data private; only publish anonymized, permissioned aggregate information.
- Review actual provider terms and controls before handling business-sensitive data.
