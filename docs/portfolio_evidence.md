## Suggested application narrative (draft; personalize before submission)
I developed an independent Small Business AI Enablement Toolkit to help nontechnical users identify repetitive work, prepare reviewable AI prompts and estimate the potential value of adoption. The project includes a browser interface, local processing, automated tests, responsible AI guidance, and a 90-minute training curriculum. I have prepared a community pilot methodology to compare time and quality and assess independent use. I am not claiming completed client deployments or measured productivity gains from this toolkit until the pilot is conducted and verified.

For the application question about a real problem already solved, use a genuine personally experienced example, not the proposed pilot. Describe what actually changed and do not invent metrics. Verify all statements and any outside-activity obligations before submitting.

## Publication gate
Retain completed participant forms, approvals and raw trials privately. Publish only authorized aggregate outcomes and separately authorized quotations. Report unfavorable results, sample size and limitations. No personal or sensitive business records belong in this repository.
