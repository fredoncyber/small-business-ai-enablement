# Community program management pilot — proposed, not executed

## Objective and scope
Evaluate whether an AI-assisted operations briefing improves preparation time and quality for a recurring community program. Select one consenting coordinator and one authorized reviewer. Use sanitized event notes, role labels, public schedules and approved facts only. Exclude personal contact information, participant identities, pastoral records, donations and other sensitive data.

## Approval gate
Before using organization information, obtain permission for the task, approved AI workspace, reviewer, private retention and deletion arrangements. Public case study, logo, screenshots and quotes require separate permission. Participation is voluntary. Stop if an unapproved data disclosure or consequential error occurs; follow the organization's incident process.

## Procedure
1. Select three comparable program-brief tasks, define required fields (objective, date, venue, action, owner, deadline, dependencies, unknowns) and agree on a common rubric.
2. Time three manual completions from approved input to reviewer-ready output, including corrections. Use a stopwatch and record privately.
3. Run three matched AI-assisted trials with the same complexity and comparable inputs; include prompt preparation, model interaction and corrections. Alternate order on distinct matched scenarios when practical to reduce learning effects. Record differences.
4. Reviewer scores outputs using the rubric before release. Do not publish unsupported claims. Record corrections, initial and final approval and any adverse events.
5. Train a second consenting coordinator for 15 minutes, then observe a new task completed independently without coaching. Record success and remaining questions.
6. Compare paired totals and report sample size, limitations, regressions and reviewer feedback. Obtain separate permission before publishing aggregate results.

## Proposed acceptance criteria (not achieved)
- Three matched pairs with complete timing and quality records.
- All released outputs fact-checked and approved; no sensitive information entered.
- At least 20% reduction in total preparation time, without a decline in quality; report failures honestly.
- Second coordinator can independently prepare and review a new brief.
- No claimed commercial cash savings; report measured time separately from hypothetical labor value.

## Quality rubric
Score each criterion 0 (missing/incorrect), 1 (partial), or 2 (complete/accurate): factual accuracy, completeness of required fields, actionable assignments, explicit unknowns, clarity. Maximum 10. Any fabricated consequential fact or unauthorized disclosure is a release blocker regardless of score. Record first-pass and final scores separately.
