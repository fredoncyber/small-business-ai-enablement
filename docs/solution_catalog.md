# Business AI solution catalog

## Demonstrable now
| Workflow | Example | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Customer inquiries | Drafting reply instructions from approved service facts | No automatic responses or booking |
| Operations planning | Checklist and staff-update instructions | No automatic task execution |
| Performance measurement | Time and estimated labor-value comparisons | Not proof of realized cash savings |

## Potential future engagements (not implemented)
Marketing content drafting, invoice/expense analysis, onboarding guidance, inventory reporting and connected workflow automation. Scope each engagement with permission, approved tools, information classification, reviewer and measurable acceptance criteria. No claims of integration or deployment before validation.

## Engagement model
Discovery → one low-risk task → prototype → review and measurement → user training → documented handover. Escalate regulated, safety-critical or high-impact decisions to qualified people.
