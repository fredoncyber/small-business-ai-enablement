# Community pilot protocol

## Scope
Pilot one low-risk workflow: event or administrative briefing, or customer reply drafting from approved public business facts. Seek organizational permission in advance. Do not present simulated work as a deployed client solution.

## Baseline
Identify a repeated task. Record at least 3 representative manual completions with task name, minutes, reviewer, quality issues, and date. Avoid storing real names or sensitive details in public artifacts.

## Pilot
Use only business-approved, sanitized content and an approved AI workspace. Record AI-assisted preparation time **including review and corrections** for comparable tasks. Have a second user independently repeat the workflow. Keep raw private records outside GitHub.

## Evaluation
Compute net minutes saved and percentage of baseline, and report both improvements and regressions. Record output accuracy (reviewer-approved / reviewed total), corrections, user confidence, and whether independent operation succeeded. Sample sizes must be disclosed; time savings are not necessarily causally attributable to AI without comparable tasks.

## Approval and publication
Obtain permission before publishing any testimonial, identifiable logo, quote, or case study. Publish only aggregated results and redacted screenshots. Never fabricate statistics or endorsements.

## Case study outline
Context → baseline task → approved solution → human controls → number of trials → measured results → errors and limitations → user training → authorized feedback → next iteration.
