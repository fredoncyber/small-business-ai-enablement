# AI for Small Business — facilitator guide
90 minutes | Beginner level | Independent portfolio project; not an official OpenAI workshop.

## Preparation
- Confirm venue, Wi-Fi, projector, accessibility needs and participant consent for any photographs or quotations. Provide a printed workbook option.
- Open the toolkit locally from web/index.html; test the public demo separately. Have a local copy and printed fictional examples as backup.
- Arrange an organization-approved AI tool for the live demonstration. Participants without access can pair up or use the example output in the answer key. No purchase is required for the workshop.
- Never collect participant passwords, private customer data, employee records or raw business inputs. Do not use participant data for live demonstrations.
- Explain that the toolkit builds prompts and estimates labor value; it does not call an AI model or send messages.

## Facilitation schedule
| Time | Facilitation activity | Participant output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Welcome, objectives, show useful and unreliable AI outputs | Name one task and one AI limitation |
| 10–25 | Map a repetitive workflow: input, steps, owner, review, time | Select one low-risk task |
| 25–45 | Demonstrate fictional customer inquiry; generate prompt, paste into approved AI tool, review against facts | Identify unknown Saturday hours |
| 45–65 | Guided operations exercise; pairs prepare prompt, compare output, assign review | Reusable prompt and checklist |
| 65–80 | Privacy mini-exercise and performance calculator | Safety checklist and illustrative savings |
| 80–90 | Teach-back, feedback, next action | Owner, date, metric and approval gate |

## Facilitator script
Opening: AI can help draft and organize information, but plausible text is not evidence. Today we will focus on tasks a person can check and reverse.
Discovery: Ask what happens repeatedly, what consumes time, what information is involved and what a correct output looks like. Choose low-risk drafting, not automated hiring, legal, medical, financial or safety decisions.
Demo: Ask the fictional business question. Show why missing hours cannot be guessed. Build the prompt, paste it into an approved AI tool, then compare the returned draft with approved facts. If the model invents an answer, identify it as an error and revise; do not hide it.
Practice: Have pairs generate a plan, then exchange roles as reviewer and operator. Require each person to explain the unknowns and approval gate.
Measurement: Compare equivalent tasks including correction time. Explain that an estimated labor value is not verified savings. A slower workflow can be the correct result.
Close: Ask participants to identify who owns the workflow, when they will test it and how they will measure success. Offer a follow-up pilot only with permission.
