## 4. Privacy exercise
Decide whether to use or remove each item: public business hours; customer credit-card number; employee medical note; public service description; private contact list; password. Discuss why source instructions cannot override task rules.

## 5. Measure value
Illustrative example: manual 30 min, assisted 15 min, 20 monthly tasks, $25 hourly labor cost, $20 tool cost. Use the calculator and explain the result. Try assisted time of 40 minutes and compare.
For a real test record comparable times including corrections. Do not claim cash savings from estimated labor value.

## 6. Independent teach-back
Without assistance, prepare a new fictional prompt, identify missing facts, explain approval and demonstrate clearing the input.
