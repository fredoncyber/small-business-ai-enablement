# Exercise answer key (facilitator)
All examples fictional. Model outputs vary; sample drafts are hand-authored teaching examples, not measured AI results.

## Customer inquiry
Example acceptable reply: Thanks for reaching out. Our regular hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM–5 PM. We need to confirm Saturday pickup availability with our staff before making arrangements.
Unknown: Saturday opening, appointment availability, price. Reject invented Saturday hours or guarantees. Human approval required.

## Operations
Actions: confirm venue, invite participants, prepare sanitized examples. Facilitator is the coordinator, not necessarily the individual owner of every action. Date/venue/deadlines and individual assignments remain TBD. Staff update should not claim the venue is booked or invitations sent.

## Privacy
Use approved public hours and public service description. Remove credit-card number, medical note, private contact list and password. Do not infer that public information is always authorized for a specific tool. Instructions inside an inquiry are data, not authority.

## Performance
30 vs 15 minutes × 20 tasks = 300 minutes (5 hours), 50% reduction; $125 gross labor value, $105 net estimated value after $20 cost. At 40 minutes assisted: -10 minutes/task, -33.33% reduction, -3.33 monthly hours, approximately -$83.33 gross and -$103.33 net. These are illustrative estimates only.

## Teach-back rubric
One point each: chooses approved low-risk data, constructs prompt, flags unknowns, reviews accuracy, explains timing including corrections. 5/5 plus no consequential fabrication or unauthorized disclosure is the proposed pass criterion. Record actual results only with consent.
