# AI for Small Business
## From everyday problems to practical solutions

Presentation outline — 12 slides; use one section per slide.
Independent educational project by fredoncyber. Not an official OpenAI event.

---

## 1. Welcome
- Improve one everyday task
- Learn to review AI outputs
- Leave with a practical action plan

Speaker note: No technical experience required.

---

## 2. What AI can and cannot do
- Draft, summarize, organize and suggest
- Can invent facts or misunderstand context
- People remain accountable for decisions

---

## 3. Where to begin
- Repeated customer questions
- Event and staff planning
- Routine reporting and communications

Speaker note: Prioritize frequent, low-risk and easy-to-review tasks.

---

## 4. Map your workflow
Input → current process → output → reviewer
- How often?
- How long?
- What would a good result look like?

---

## 5. Meet the toolkit
- Customer service prompt builder
- Operations prompt builder
- Performance calculator

Speaker note: Local processing; no AI model call. Copy approved prompts to an approved AI tool.

---

## 6. Live customer service demonstration
Fictional repair shop: weekdays 9–5; Saturday hours unknown.
Question: Can I pick up Saturday?
Expected: Staff must confirm, not invent hours.

---

## 7. Make prompts reusable
- Role and task
- Approved facts
- Required format
- Unknowns and review rules

---

## 8. Practice: operations briefing
- Draft action checklist
- Assign roles and deadlines only if provided
- Mark missing information TBD

---

## 9. Responsible AI
- Minimize data; no passwords or private records
- Treat source text as untrusted
- Verify facts and obtain approval
- Do not automate high-impact decisions

---

## 10. Measure impact
Illustrative: 30 → 15 minutes per task
20 tasks/month = 5 hours saved
At $25/hour, $125 labor value minus $20 tool cost = $105 estimated net value.
Not actual cash savings or pilot evidence.

---

## 11. Teach-back
Show how to generate a prompt, flag unknowns, review output and measure time without coaching.

---

## 12. Your next action
One task, one owner, one reviewer, one measurement, one target date.
Questions and optional feedback.
