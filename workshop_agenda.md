# AI for Small Business — 90-minute workshop
**Audience:** Owners and employees with little technical experience
**Goal:** Each participant leaves with a reviewable, reusable prompt for a real low-risk task.

| Minutes | Activity | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | What AI can and cannot do | Realistic expectations |
| 10–25 | Identify repetitive tasks | Selected use case |
| 25–45 | Live customer response demonstration | Draft and fact-check |
| 45–65 | Guided participant practice | Reusable prompt |
| 65–80 | Privacy and human review | Safety checklist |
| 80–90 | Action plan and questions | Owner and metric for next step |

Facilitator demonstration must use fictional data. Ask participants to avoid entering sensitive information into unapproved tools.
